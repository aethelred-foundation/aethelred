# M42 Deal Map — Customer Decision Architecture

The technology exists; the missing piece is a **customer decision architecture**. This document answers the six questions and maps the buying committee.

## The six questions

1. **What M42 business decision will the pilot enable?** → *Should M42 deploy Aethelred as the compute‑assurance layer for a defined class of AI workloads?*
2. **Which M42 executive owns that decision?** → business/digital sponsor (see committee) + economic buyer.
3. **What economic/operational result is measured?** → verified unit cost, recoverable GPU‑hours, audit‑time saved, sustainability‑reporting effort avoided, risk reduction (built on M42 baselines in Phase 0/1).
4. **What risks does Aethelred absorb?** → phase gates + stop/go after deployment; contingency workload if a dependency is delayed; useful exit package even without expansion.
5. **What does M42 receive even if it does not proceed?** → independently operable verifier, verified baseline, attestation/measurement report, optimization findings, security & DPIA pack, production business case.
6. **What happens after successful acceptance?** → jointly worded technical validation statement + a conditional production deployment plan (LOI); investment handled separately.

## The decision chain (mirror M42's own language)

> **M42 priority → current operational problem → selected workflow → Aethelred intervention → measurable evidence → executive decision → production expansion.**

## Buying committee (role‑based hypothesis — confirm against reality)

| Required role | Publicly relevant M42 leadership (hypothesis) | What they must believe |
|---|---|---|
| Business / digital sponsor | Group Chief Digital Officer / Digital Health Solutions lead | Improves AI operations and scales beyond one demo |
| Integrated data / genomics sponsor | Integrated Health Solutions lead | Supports sovereign, governed use of M42's data infrastructure |
| Clinical assurance | Group CMO + selected workload's clinical lead | Aethelred does not overstate clinical validation or interfere with clinical accountability |
| Economic buyer | Platform leadership + CFO org | Production economics justify the engagement and expansion |
| Legal / privacy | Group GC, Group DPO, platform privacy officer | Data, claims, contracts, and processing responsibilities are controlled |
| Security / infrastructure | M42 security, cloud, platform owners | Attestation, keys, deployment, telemetry, supply‑chain risks are acceptable |
| Strategy / transformation | Group Strategy & Transformation | The pilot creates a reusable capability, not a one‑off demo |
| Procurement | Sourcing / vendor management | Scope, acceptance, support, liability, pricing are clear |

**Do not try to sell the whole committee in one meeting.** Secure four first: **(1) a business champion, (2) a technical champion, (3) an economic owner, (4) early security/privacy participation.**

## Four separated commercial lanes

| Lane | Instrument | M42 commits | Aethelred receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid client | MSA/SOW | fee, access, environment | revenue + real‑world deployment |
| Founding design partner | collaboration schedule / MOU | feedback + steering | roadmap validation |
| Technical validator | pre‑agreed protocol + post‑pilot statement | objective review **only after** criteria pass | approved validation wording |
| Potential investor | **separate** strategic process | none inside the SOW | optional post‑milestone conversation |

**Ask:** a jointly approved technical validation statement, conditional on acceptance — *not* a guaranteed endorsement, *not* "M42‑certified". If M42 also invests, use separate technical and investment decision groups to protect validation independence.

## Commercial protections (in the SOW)

Milestone payments · stop/go gate after deployment · pilot‑fee credit toward production · 12‑month price protection · founding design‑partner status · no blanket exclusivity · no surprise integration fee · exit package even without expansion.
