# Whitepaper Comparison Audit — March 2026 (Historical)

> **Status: HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED.** These reports compared the then-current
> detailed-numbers tokenomics in `WHITEPAPER.md` (v1.1) against the
> `Aethelred_Whitepaper_v2.0.pdf` and flagged 14 material discrepancies
> (vesting schedules, cliffs, TGE unlock percentages). They were produced on
> **2026-03-11** and are retained here as an audit-trail record, not as live
> findings.

## Why they are superseded

In April 2026 the public disclosure documents were restructured into
**posture-based "Public Canonical Draft" documents** ([`docs/WHITEPAPER.md`](../../WHITEPAPER.md),
[`docs/TOKENOMICS.md`](../../TOKENOMICS.md)). The current public documents
**deliberately withhold** the specific vesting schedules, cliffs, and TGE
unlock percentages that were the subject of these discrepancies — under the
stated rule that *"governed legal, commercial, and technical disclosures
publish only when approved for release."*

As a result, the specific numeric conflicts these reports describe **no longer
appear in the public documents**: the conflict was resolved by moving the
disputed specifics out of public disclosure pending legal review, not by
reconciling two conflicting public figures.

## Residual item (for document control / legal review)

One non-numeric item remains a document-control decision, **not** an
engineering fix:

- `docs/WHITEPAPER.md` is labelled **Version 1.1** (dated 2026-04-03) while a
  **v2.0** PDF exists. Which version is authoritative for ADGM submission is a
  call for the appointed legal reviewer (the document's own "Legal Reviewer"
  control field is still unassigned).
