# M42 $1M-Value Pilot Structure

Status date: 2026-06-11

## Intent

The M42 pilot fee is $200,000, but the pilot should feel like a $1,000,000 strategic decision package. The value target is not a guaranteed savings claim. It is the operating design for giving M42 reusable assets, executive confidence, and next-phase clarity that are worth far more than a simple technical demo.

## Value Architecture

| Value Bank | Target Value | What M42 Receives |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Sovereign AI assurance blueprint | $250,000 | Dedicated UAE/Abu Dhabi sandbox topology, data-boundary controls, archive, monitoring, go-live gates, and a reusable path for the next private healthcare AI workload |
| Verifiable clinical AI evidence pack | $250,000 | Per-case hybrid TEE + zkML + Digital Seal evidence bundles that technical, security, and clinical AI reviewers can inspect |
| Security and procurement acceleration | $200,000 | Security model, negative controls, audit export shape, operational health checks, and evidence objects packaged for internal risk review |
| Clinical safety protocol | $150,000 | Synthetic Med42-compatible evaluation protocol with factuality, safety flag recall, escalation behavior, and adverse-mode rejection measurement |
| Scale decision business case | $150,000 | Executive readout, economics comparison, next-phase options, and go/no-go criteria for Med42 expansion, retrospective clinical AI, or genomics follow-on |

Target value total: $1,000,000.

## Premium Pilot Experience

| Moment | Timing | Purpose | Output |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|
| Sponsor alignment session | Before Week 1 clock starts | Confirm success definition, decision owners, workload boundary, and evidence contract | Signed-off pilot scorecard and mutual action plan |
| Evidence room walkthrough | Week 2 | Let M42 inspect an accepted job from prompt to model hash, TEE evidence, zkML object, Digital Seal, archive, and metrics | Per-case evidence bundle review |
| Assurance challenge session | Week 3 | Run the negative controls and show the system rejecting bad evidence, wrong hashes, PHI injection, fallback mode, and replay | Negative-control results and security action log |
| Executive value board | Week 4 | Convert technical results into a scale decision and business case | Value scorecard, executive readout, next-phase recommendation |

## What Makes It Feel Worth 5x

- M42 leaves with reusable infrastructure, not just screenshots.
- M42 receives inspectable evidence for every accepted synthetic case, not only aggregate metrics.
- M42 gets a clinical, security, procurement, and executive packet from one four-week effort.
- The pilot produces a clear next-phase decision: expand Med42, move to de-identified retrospective clinical AI, select a genomics workload, or stop.
- The evidence contract rejects fallback paths, so the paid result does not depend on weaker TEE-only or commitments-only proof.

## Acceptance Bar

The pilot is successful only if the live sandbox produces:

- 100% hybrid TEE + zkML + Digital Seal coverage for accepted cases.
- 0 single-evidence fallback accepted cases.
- 0 live PHI/customer records introduced without written scope change.
- 6 of 6 negative controls rejected.
- A completed value-bank scorecard reviewed with the M42 sponsor.
- A go/no-go recommendation with owners, risks, and next-phase budget logic.

## Conversion Path

The value architecture exists to be converted, not admired. The acceptance bar above maps directly to the preconditions in the LOI draft, so a passed pilot flows into signature rather than a new negotiation. The full two-track design (LOI/MOU at the Week 4 value board; $2M pre-seed via the corporate development channel) is specified in `docs/operations/m42-conversion-plan.md`. The pilot is never contingent on the investment, and the investment is never a condition of pilot success.
