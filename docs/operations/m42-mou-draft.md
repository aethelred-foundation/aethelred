# Memorandum of Understanding (Draft) — M42 × Aethelred Strategic Collaboration

Status date: 2026-06-12
Document status: DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. Working draft prepared by Aethelred for discussion. Not a legal instrument; not reviewed by counsel; creates no obligations. Bracketed fields are open decision points.

Use this instrument instead of the LOI (`m42-loi-draft.md`) when M42 prefers a strategic-partnership frame without committing to a next-phase commercial range. ## Memorandum of Understanding

**Between:** [M42 legal entity name] ("M42")
**And:** [Aethelred Labs Ltd, in formation under ADGM] ("Aethelred")
**Date:** [____], 2026

### 1. Purpose

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") records the parties' shared intent to collaborate on sovereign, verifiable AI compute infrastructure for regulated healthcare workloads, following the four-week paid evaluation pilot completed on [date].

### 2. Shared Objectives

2.1. Establish independently verifiable evidence — TEE attestation, zkML proof, and Digital Seal — as the assurance standard for M42's regulated AI workloads sold to sovereign and enterprise buyers.

2.2. Keep sensitive health and genomic data within approved jurisdictional boundaries while making results verifiable across borders.

2.3. Position the UAE as the reference deployment for sovereign verified AI compute in healthcare.

### 3. Areas of Collaboration

The parties intend to explore, without obligation:

a. **Next-phase workloads.** Expanded Med42 evaluation, de-identified retrospective clinical AI, or genomics workloads under the pilot's evidence contract, each subject to its own statement of work.

b. **Joint working group.** A standing technical working group ([quarterly / monthly]) covering evidence schema evolution, regulatory alignment (UAE data protection, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001), and workload prioritization.

c. **Validator participation.** Evaluation of M42 operating validator capacity on its approved infrastructure under Aethelred's Proof of Useful Work design, subject to the independence conditions described in the business case (no single party controls consensus; rewards structured under the ADGM virtual-asset framework with tax and accounting review).

d. **Reference and co-marketing.** Subject to Section 6, jointly approved case studies or reference materials describing the pilot methodology and evidence model.

### 4. Strategic Investment Acknowledgment

The parties acknowledge that discussions regarding a potential strategic investment by [M42 / its corporate development affiliate] in Aethelred may proceed in parallel. Such discussions are independent of this MOU, are not a condition of any collaboration described here, and will be governed by their own documentation and approvals.

### 5. Status and Non-Binding Effect

5.1. This MOU is a statement of intent. Except for Sections 6 (announcements and confidentiality) and 7 (costs), it is non-binding and creates no legal obligation, partnership, agency, or joint venture.

5.2. Any binding engagement requires definitive agreements executed after Aethelred's ADGM entity registration completes and after each party's internal approvals and legal review.

5.3. No M42 production system shall depend on Aethelred infrastructure under this MOU.

### 6. Announcements and Confidentiality

6.1. Neither party will make public announcements about this MOU or the collaboration, or use the other's name or marks, without prior written approval.

6.2. Pilot artifacts, evidence bundles, and the contents of this MOU remain confidential under [existing NDA dated ____ / terms to be agreed].

### 7. Costs

Each party bears its own costs.

### 8. Term and Review

This MOU takes effect on signature and continues for [12] months unless extended in writing or superseded by definitive agreements. The parties will review progress at the joint working group every [quarter].

### 9. Governing Framework

This MOU is to be read under the laws of [the Abu Dhabi Global Market].
